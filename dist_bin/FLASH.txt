np2_espresso - prebuilt firmware (final distribution build)
============================================================

Target: ESP32-S3 (N16R8 / 16MB flash / 8MB Octal PSRAM)
Build : build 20260814 (LCD SPI clock selectable, 40MHz default; RGB444 output)

Flash with esptool (replace PORT, e.g. COM21 or /dev/ttyACM0):

  esptool --chip esp32s3 -p PORT -b 460800 --before default_reset --after hard_reset \
    write_flash --flash_mode dio --flash_size 16MB --flash_freq 80m \
    0x0     bootloader.bin \
    0x8000  partition-table.bin \
    0xe000  ota_data_initial.bin \
    0x10000 np2_espresso.bin

Before running, put BIOS.ROM / SOUND.ROM / FONT.ROM and your .NHD/.NFD disk
images on the microSD card root (see ../README.md / ../README.en.md).

To rebuild from source instead, use ../build.sh (requires ESP-IDF v5.5).
